# 🏙️ CivicVoice

### Your Voice. Your City.

**CivicVoice** is an AI-assisted civic issue reporting platform that helps citizens report problems in their neighborhoods and gives them a transparent way to track what happens next.

From potholes and overflowing garbage to water leaks, broken streetlights, and unsafe public areas, CivicVoice transforms a simple citizen complaint into a structured, location-aware civic report.

> **Report it. Map it. Track it. Resolve it.**

# 🚨 The Problem

Every city faces everyday civic problems that affect communities:

- 🕳️ Potholes and damaged roads
- 🗑️ Garbage accumulation
- 💧 Water leaks
- 💡 Broken streetlights
- 🚧 Damaged public infrastructure
- ⚠️ Unsafe or hazardous areas
- 🏚️ Neglected civic spaces

However, reporting these problems can be fragmented, slow, and difficult to track.

Citizens may not know:

- Where to report an issue
- Which department should handle it
- Whether someone else has already reported it
- What happens after submitting a complaint
- Whether the issue is actually being addressed

### CivicVoice brings the entire journey into one platform.
