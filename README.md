# CivicVoice

### Your Voice. Your City.

**CivicVoice** is an AI-assisted civic issue reporting platform that helps citizens report problems in their neighborhoods and gives communities a transparent way to track what happens next.

From potholes and overflowing garbage to water leaks, broken streetlights, and unsafe public areas, CivicVoice turns a simple citizen complaint into a structured, location-aware civic report.

> **Report it. Map it. Track it. Resolve it.**

---

##  The Problem

Every city has everyday problems that affect thousands of people:

- 🕳️ Potholes and damaged roads
- 🗑️ Garbage accumulation
- 💧 Water leaks
- 💡 Broken streetlights
- 🚧 Damaged public infrastructure
- ⚠️ Unsafe or hazardous areas
- 🏚️ Neglected civic spaces

But reporting these issues can be fragmented, slow, and difficult to track.

Citizens often don't know:

- Where to report an issue
- Which department should handle it
- Whether someone else already reported it
- What happened after submitting a complaint
- Whether the issue is actually being resolved

### CivicVoice brings the entire journey into one platform.

---

#  Our Solution

CivicVoice creates a simple civic feedback loop:

```text
CITIZEN
   ↓
VOICE / TEXT REPORT
   ↓
AI-ASSISTED ANALYSIS
   ↓
CATEGORY + SEVERITY + SUMMARY
   ↓
LOCATION DETECTION
   ↓
MAP + PUBLIC REPORT
   ↓
ADMIN / AUTHORITY ACTION
   ↓
IN PROGRESS
   ↓
RESOLVED
